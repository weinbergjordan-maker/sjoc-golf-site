Tournament Format

  • Day 1: 36 holes. Best Ball. Lunch break. Foursomes/Alternate Shot.
  • Evening 1: Wedge Wars, etc.
  • Day 2: 18 holes. Match Play. Trophy Presentation and Awards.

  • John Daly/Bruce Koepka Long Drive: Must be in fairway
  • Grass Whisperer: Long putt
  • Trunk Slammer: Best 1st hole score
  • David Hasselhoff Sandie: Most time in sand
  • Most Room on the Left: Best recovery hole on errant drive (Par Needed)
  • Most Room on the Right: Best recovery hole on errant drive (Par Needed)
  • Johnny Miller "Impossible Shot": Single best recovery shot (any score)

  • Thorbjorn Olesen: Jabroni award strictly related to travel to/from
  • Savage Team: Winning Team
  • Payne Poley Stewart "You're Gonna be a Father": Most poleys (putts made outside the length of flagpole)
